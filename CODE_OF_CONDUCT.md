# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Protect users' private account data.
- Avoid publishing usernames, IDs, cookies, tokens, or credentials.
- Respect the read-only and privacy-first scope of the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Encouraging credential theft, token collection, or unauthorized account access.
- Submitting hidden, obfuscated, or externally loaded executable code.
- Pressuring maintainers to add automated account modification or safeguard circumvention.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns through a private channel when possible. Do not include sensitive account data in public reports.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
